    Research mode

    Records what is said for human-robot interaction research, then run Research session. No names or faces are stored with it. Switching this on is the consent — the robot does not ask out loud, so take consent from participants before you start.

    This is not ethics approval. Trinity requires approval before collecting data from participants. This only makes the robot able to do it properly once you have that.

    Recordings

    Transcripts, not audio. No sound is stored at any point — speech is turned into text and the recording is discarded immediately, so a session here cannot be traced back to a voice. That is deliberate, and it is why there is nothing to play back.
